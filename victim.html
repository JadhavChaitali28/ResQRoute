🩸 How to Stop Severe Bleeding

Apply firm, direct pressure on the wound using a clean cloth. Keep holding firm pressure continuously until rescuers arrive. Elevate the injured limb above heart level if possible.

🌊 Flood Water Safety Rules

Never walk through moving water deeper than your ankles. Stay on higher floors/rooftops if trapped. Avoid touching submerged electrical equipment or wires.
